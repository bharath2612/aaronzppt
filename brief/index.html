Five years of Dubai Land Department sales. The value of property sold went from AED 71.7B in 2020 to AED 680B in 2025, about ten times. 2020 was the pandemic year, so it is a low starting point, but every year since has grown. The 2021 and 2022 figures are not in the DLD reports we have, so we estimated them from DLD transaction data, adding up every sales registration; that method matches DLD's reported totals for 2023, 2024 and 2025 within 0.5%. Completed property sales rose from AED 1.0B in 2020 to AED 3.2B in 2025, more than three times. 2024 peaked at AED 6.4B, lifted by AED 2.65B of tourism land sold in ten deals. 2026 to September is AED 2.1B, already 67% of the 2025 total in nine months; the dashed box shows the AED 1.1B still needed to match 2025. This is the commission developers pay on off-plan sales, the money brokerages wait for. At a conservative 4%, off-plan sales across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Ras Al Khaimah carried about AED 14.5B of commission in 2025 and AED 12.2B so far in 2026, AED 26.7B in total. 03Section

The problem

03The problem · Brokers

Brokers live on commission, and wait months for it

  1. 01

    No loan facility against the commission they are owed

  2. 02

    No salary: they live from one commission to the next

  3. 03

    Less motivation to sell more while earlier commissions are unpaid

Brokers have no salary; they live from one commission to the next. There is no loan facility against commission they are owed, and while earlier commissions are unpaid there is little motivation to sell more.

03The problem · Brokerages

Brokerages cannot plan around late, uncertain payments

  1. 01

    Cash flow gaps limit campaigns and marketing

  2. 02

    Late payments put their best brokers at risk of leaving

  3. 03

    Revenue and growth numbers are unpredictable

Brokerages face cash flow gaps that limit their campaigns and marketing. Late payments put their best brokers at risk of leaving, and revenue and growth become unpredictable.

03The problem · Developers

Late payments cost developers reputation and sales

  1. 01

    Paying brokers late, under cash flow pressure, damages their reputation

  2. 02

    Slow payments slow the sales cycle, as brokers lose motivation

  3. 03

    Invoicing is manual, with no digital view of who is owed what, and when

Developers under cash flow pressure pay brokers late, which damages their reputation with the broker market. Slow payments slow the sales cycle because brokers lose motivation to push the project. Invoicing is manual and paper-heavy, with no digital view of who is owed what, and when.
