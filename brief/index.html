03Section

The problem

03The problem · Brokers

Brokers live on commission, and wait months for it

  1. 01

    No loan facility against the commission they are owed

  2. 02

    No salary: they live from one commission to the next

  3. 03

    Less motivation to sell more while earlier commissions are unpaid

  4. 04

    Trust is missing

Brokers have no salary; they live from one commission to the next. There is no loan facility against commission they are owed, and while earlier commissions are unpaid there is little motivation to sell more. Trust between brokers, brokerages and developers is missing.

03The problem · Brokerages

Brokerages cannot plan around late, uncertain payments

  1. 01

    Cash flow gaps limit campaigns, marketing and the day-to-day running of the business

  2. 02

    Late payments put their best brokers at risk of leaving, as brokers assume the brokerage is not paying or not effective

  3. 03

    Revenue and growth numbers are unpredictable

  4. 04

    Managing a business on cash predictions is not a business

Brokerages face cash flow gaps that limit campaigns, marketing and how the business runs. Late payments put their best brokers at risk of leaving, because brokers assume the brokerage is not paying or not effective. Revenue and growth become unpredictable, and a business managed on cash predictions is not a real business.

03The problem · Developers

Late payments cost developers reputation and sales

  1. 01

    Paying brokers late damages their reputation

  2. 02

    Slow payments slow the sales cycle, as brokers lose motivation

  3. 03

    Invoicing is manual, with no digital view of who is owed what, and when

  4. 04

    No fin-real-tech solution is available to take on this challenge

Paying brokers late damages a developer's reputation with the broker market. Slow payments slow the sales cycle because brokers lose motivation to push the project. Invoicing is manual, with no digital view of who is owed what, and when. And no fin-real-tech solution is available to take this on.
