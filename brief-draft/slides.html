Example figures: a 1.5% charge for about 50 days is about 11% a year. The rate, the split in Option 2 and AaronzPay's share of the charge are still to be agreed with the finance partner.

Paying a brokerage at registration instead of about 50 days later has a cost, and someone has to pay it. Take a AED 100,000 commission and a 1.5% charge. In Option 1 the brokerage pays: it receives AED 98,500 at registration, and the developer pays the usual AED 100,000 on its usual date, so nothing changes for the developer. In Option 2 the brokerage and the developer share the charge: the brokerage receives AED 99,250 and the developer pays AED 100,750. Either way the finance partner earns the same AED 1,500 and pays AaronzPay its share. These are example figures; the rate, the split and AaronzPay's share are still to be agreed.

Status: to be built in the platform; to be agreed in the contracts; decision needed between AaronzPay and the finance partner.

The finance partner's risk is simple to describe: the developer owes the money, for about 50 days, on a commission the developer has already confirmed at registration. Protection works in three stages. Before paying, the platform checks that the brokerage, broker, project and marketing agreement are registered, pays only on a confirmed registration and only once per unit, and the finance partner approves each developer and its limit. While waiting, the developer has accepted that the finance partner collects the commission, and exposure to each developer stays under its limit. If something goes wrong, two rules are still to be decided: if a sale is cancelled, the developer still repays; and if the developer does not pay, either the brokerage repays or the finance partner carries the loss.
