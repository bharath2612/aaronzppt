07Pricing

The charge is the same. The choice is who pays it.

Example: a AED 100,000 commission, paid at registration instead of about 50 days later, for a 1.5% charge.

Brokerage · AED 750Developer · AED 750

The developer pays AED 750 more, for brokers paid at registration.

Example figures: a 1.5% charge for about 50 days is about 11% a year. The rate, the split in Option 2 and AaronzPay's share of the charge are still to be agreed with the finance partner.

Paying a brokerage at registration instead of about 50 days later has a cost, and someone has to pay it. Take a AED 100,000 commission and a 1.5% charge. In Option 1 the brokerage pays: it receives AED 98,500 at registration, and the developer pays the usual AED 100,000 on its usual date, so nothing changes for the developer. In Option 2 the brokerage and the developer share the charge: the brokerage receives AED 99,250 and the developer pays AED 100,750. Either way the finance partner earns the same AED 1,500 and pays AaronzPay its share. These are example figures; the rate, the split and AaronzPay's share are still to be agreed.

The finance partner's exposure is to the developer, for about 50 days, on a commission the developer has already confirmed at registration. This slide lists the risks a credit team will ask about and the control for each. If the developer pays late or not at all, the developer's confirmation and its acceptance that the finance partner collects the commission are the main protection, with a limit for each developer. If the developer still does not pay, we need to decide whether the brokerage repays the finance partner or the finance partner carries the loss and prices it in. If a sale is cancelled after payout, the developer agreement should say the developer still repays and settles any refund with the brokerage. Before every payment the platform checks that the brokerage, broker, project and marketing agreement are registered, pays only against a confirmed registration, and pays only once per unit. The finance partner approves each developer and sets its limit.
