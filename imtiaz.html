Appendix

Questions a finance team will ask

What if the sale is cancelled?

We advance only after the SPA is signed and funds have cleared. If the commission is reversed, Aaronz recovers it from the broker. Imtiaz owes nothing extra.

Does this create debt for Imtiaz?

No. Imtiaz pays a commission it already owes, on its normal date. There's no loan, guarantee or recourse to Imtiaz.

What if Aaronz stops operating?

The arrangement ends and Imtiaz pays brokers directly again. Early payouts already made are settled under the agreement.

Who sets the broker's fee?

Aaronz, shown in AED before the broker accepts. Imtiaz can review the pricing and the wording.

What data do you need?

Only per-sale eligibility: unit, broker, commission amount, SPA status and cleared funds. No buyer financial data.

How is this regulated?

Aaronz is pursuing a DIFC (DFSA) credit licence. The pilot goes live only once the required permission and funding line are in place.
