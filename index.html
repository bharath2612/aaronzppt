Verification, funding & settlement

Verify the invoice, release the advance, then collect and distribute the developer’s payment.

  1. 01

    Brokerage registers

    Identity, business details, licence and payment account are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Brokerage submits the invoice

    Include sale documents, commission agreement and milestone evidence.

  3. 03

    Developer confirms the receivable

    Confirm invoice amount, payment conditions and expected due date.

  4. 04

    Funding partner approves

    Agree advance rate, customer fee, collection route and recourse terms.

  5. 05

    Funds reach the brokerage

    The partner disburses the approved advance, less agreed fees.

Collection & settlement

  1. Developer pays ArronzThe confirmed commission is paid into the agreed Arronz collection account.
  2. Arronz repays the bankSettle the funded principal, accrued interest and agreed bank charges.
  3. Arronz retains its earningsKeep the agreed customer fee less funding costs. Upfront fee deductions are accounted for once.
  4. Arronz pays the customerRelease the remaining commission balance to the brokerage after reconciling the advance and fees.

If verification fails: request missing evidence or decline with a reason.

Confirm the commission
and payment period

The developer’s agreement may mention only the base commission, with additional commission based on the ongoing offer.

Confirm the invoice amount and agree a collection period with the developer. This period determines our fixed fee per invoice.
What it means to us

Work on an
invoice-by-invoice basis

Developers may not pay the entire commission in one go. Payments may be released in stages, so an invoice can cover part or all of the commission.

Once the brokerage is eligible to raise an invoice and the developer confirms it, we can advance against that invoice amount.

Developer onboarding strategy

Agree payment terms with each developer before accepting their commission invoices.

Challenges

Why payments may be delayed

Developers may delay broker payments because of cash-flow pressure, the pace of property sales or market conditions.

These delays make the commission payment date harder to predict.

What it means to us

Agree a payment window

  1. Onboard each developer and agree a payment period, such as 60 or 90 days, with a clear start date.
  2. Accept invoices only from developers already onboarded to Arronz, subject to invoice verification.
  3. If payment is late, the developer pays the agreed daily interest on the overdue amount, starting the day after the payment window ends.

04

EIBOR + bank margin

EIBOR is the UAE interbank benchmark interest rate. Six-month EIBOR was 4.58991% on 1 October 2026. Adding the 2-percentage-point bank margin used in FAB’s example gives 6.58991% (about 6.59%) annually. This is an illustration, not a bank quote. FAB also lists a 0.15% invoice charge, minimum AED 250.
