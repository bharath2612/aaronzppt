Problem

Agents and brokerages can close a sale but wait months for developer commission. The receivable is earned, yet operating cash remains locked.

Product

Purchase the pending commission receivable, advance most of it immediately, and collect from the broker/developer when the commission is paid.

Why now

UAE real-estate sales volumes are large, digital verification is improving, and the dedicated commission-finance competitive set is still thin.

Source: Dubai Land Department · 9 March 2026 ↗. This is Dubai’s commission pool, not a UAE total or fully eligible financing volume.

Verification, funding & settlement

Verify the invoice, release the advance, then collect and distribute the developer’s payment.

Verification

  1. Brokerage registers

    Review identity, business details, licence and payment account.

  2. Brokerage submits the invoice

    Include sale documents, commission agreement and milestone evidence.

  3. Developer confirms the receivable

    Confirm invoice amount, payment conditions and expected due date.

If verification fails: request missing evidence or decline with a reason.

Funding

  1. Funding partner approves

    Agree advance rate, customer fee, collection route and recourse terms.

  2. Funds reach the brokerage

    The partner disburses the approved advance, less agreed fees.

Settlement

  1. Developer pays Aaronz

    The confirmed commission is paid into the agreed Aaronz collection account.

  2. Aaronz repays the bank

    Settle the funded principal, accrued interest and agreed bank charges.

  3. Aaronz retains its earnings

    Keep the customer fee less funding costs. Count upfront fee deductions only once.

  4. Aaronz pays the customer

    Pay the remaining commission balance to the brokerage after reconciling the advance and fees.

Proposed Aaronz workflow. Funding and collection arrangements require agreement with the financing partner and counterparties.

invoice-by-invoice basis

Developers may not pay the entire commission in one go. Payments may be released in stages, so an invoice can cover part or all of the commission.

Once the brokerage is eligible to raise an invoice and the developer confirms it, we can advance against that invoice amount.

Developer onboarding strategy

Agree payment terms with each developer before accepting their commission invoices.

Challenges

Why payments may be delayed

Developers may delay broker payments because of cash-flow pressure, the pace of property sales or market conditions.

These delays make the commission payment date harder to predict.

What it means to us

Agree a payment window

  1. Onboard each developer and agree a payment period, such as 60 or 90 days, with a clear start date.
  2. Accept invoices only from developers already onboarded to Aaronz, subject to invoice verification.
  3. If payment is late, the developer pays the agreed daily interest on the overdue amount, starting the day after the payment window ends.
