Where the cash arrives

The sale and commission invoice stay the same. The timing of the brokerage’s cash receipt changes.

Verification, funding & settlement

Verify the invoice, release the advance, then collect and distribute the developer’s payment.

  1. 01

    Brokerage registers

    Identity, business details, licence and payment account are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Brokerage submits the invoice

    Include sale documents, commission agreement and milestone evidence.

  3. 03

    Developer confirms the receivable

    Confirm invoice amount, payment conditions and expected due date.

  4. 04

    Funding partner approves

    Agree advance rate, customer fee, collection route and recourse terms.

  5. 05

    Funds reach the brokerage

    The partner disburses the approved advance, less agreed fees.

Collection & settlement

  1. Developer pays ArronzThe confirmed commission is paid into the agreed Arronz collection account.
  2. Arronz repays the bankSettle the funded principal, accrued interest and agreed bank charges.
  3. Arronz retains its earningsKeep the agreed customer fee less funding costs. Upfront fee deductions are accounted for once.
  4. Arronz pays the customerRelease the remaining commission balance to the brokerage after reconciling the advance and fees.

If verification fails: request missing evidence or decline with a reason.

Proposed Arronz workflow. Funding and collection arrangements require agreement with the financing partner and counterparties.
