02The problem

Brokerages wait to be paid

The sale is done, but the commission is still several steps away.

The sale

Booking is done

The brokerage has made the sale. Nothing has happened on its commission yet.

The steps

Then the paperwork

A milestone is reached, the developer notifies the brokerage, and the brokerage raises an invoice.

The wait

Paid on the developer's terms

The payment date follows the developer's payment terms. The brokerage has no say in it.

03The solution

The brokerage chooses the date

Aaronz sits between developer and brokerage, and pays every commission.

Brokerage

Paid exactly on the chosen day

Same day, 15 days, 30 days or the usual date. Early dates carry a fee; the usual date is free.

Developer

Pays as usual

The developer keeps its payment dates and pays all commission to Aaronz.

Aaronz

Handles every payment

Aaronz verifies each invoice, then pays the brokerage on the date it selected.

Open with the promise: punctuality. The brokerage knows the exact date it will be paid.
