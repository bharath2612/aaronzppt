Indicative timeline.

The commission milestone is achieved, it takes 4 to 5 days for the developer to process it and tell the brokerage to invoice, then about 30 to 45 days for the money to arrive.

03The solution

Paid the same day

Aaronz pays the commission, and the developer still pays on its usual dates. Brokerages can also choose a later date.

  1. Day 0

    Commission milestone achieved

    The booking is done.

  2. Immediately

    “Now you can withdraw”

    Aaronz notifies the brokerage straight away.

  3. Same day

    Money withdrawn

    The brokerage raises its invoice and withdraws the same day.

Milestone achieved. Money withdrawn the same day.

Proposed timeline. Invoices raised after the daily cut-off are paid the next working day.

Compare with the last slide: roughly 2 months becomes the same day. The promise is same day for invoices raised before the daily cut-off; later ones are paid the next working day.

07Who we partner with

Handpicked developers only

We only work with developers who have a clean financial track record and pay their dues on time. Our internal tools test each record and tell us whether to fund or partner.

Reputation

Their standing in the market.

Social reputation

What people say about them publicly.

Banking history

Their record with their banks.

Balance sheets

The strength of their finances.

Leverage

How leveraged they are.

Considering all of these, we make a call.

Handpicked is about the record, not the size of the developer: any developer with a clean financial track record can qualify. Emphasise the track record of paying dues on time. We have built internal tools that test a developer's record and tell us whether to fund or partner. We look at reputation, social reputation, banking history, balance sheets and leverage, then make a call.
