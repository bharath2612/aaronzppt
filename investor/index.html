02The problem

Roughly 2 months to get paid

The commission is earned, but the money is still weeks away, and both sides pay for it.

  1. Commission milestone achieved

    The booking is done.

  2. 4 to 5 days

    “Now you can raise an invoice”

    The developer processes it, then tells the brokerage.

  3. 30 to 45 days

    Money in the bank

    The brokerage waits for payment.

Brokerages

Can't plan or grow

Salaries and rent are due every month, with no exact date for the commission.

Agents

Stuck with pending commission

Agents who want to change agency can't move freely while their commission is still pending.

Developers

Carry the admin, lose broker attention

Finance teams process every invoice and field the chasing. Brokerages may push projects from developers who pay faster.

The risk

Commission isn't safe until it's paid

Until the money lands, the brokerage carries the risk of delays, disputes or a developer that can't pay.

Indicative timeline.

The commission milestone is achieved, it takes 4 to 5 days for the developer to process it and tell the brokerage to invoice, then about 30 to 45 days for the money to arrive. The timeline is only the symptom. Brokerages can't plan or grow, agents who want to change agency are stuck with pending commission, developers carry the admin and may lose broker attention to faster payers, and the brokerage carries the risk until the money lands. That risk is why we test every developer before we sign. Use real examples from brokerages and developers here if you have them.

03The solution

Paid the same day

Aaronz pays the commission, and the developer still pays on its usual dates. Brokerages can also choose a later date.

  1. Commission milestone achieved

    The booking is done.

  2. Immediately

    “Now you can withdraw”

    Aaronz notifies the brokerage straight away.

  3. Same day

    Money withdrawn

    The brokerage raises its invoice and withdraws the same day.

Brokerages

Can plan and grow

They know the exact day the money arrives, so salaries and rent are covered on time.

Agents

Free to move

Commission is paid the same day, so nothing pending ties an agent to their agency.

Developers

Less admin, more broker attention

Aaronz handles the invoices and the chasing. Brokers paid the same day have a reason to push their projects.

The risk

Money in the bank the same day

Once paid, the brokerage no longer waits on the developer. Aaronz collects later, only from developers it has tested.

Proposed timeline. Settled the same day if the invoice is raised before 2 pm; after that, the next working day.

07Who we partner with

Every developer tested before we sign

We start with reputed developers that hold a large share of the market. Our internal tools then test each one on five things.

Will we get paid?

Reputation

Well regarded by brokers and buyers.

Cash flow

Strong finances and manageable debt.

Payment record

A proven history of paying dues on time.

Is the volume there?

Market share

A large share of sales in the market.

Unsold inventory

Stock still to sell, so the volume keeps coming.

If they don't pass all five, we don't sign.

Aaronz pays the brokerage first and collects from the developer later. The first three tests protect our money: will they stand behind it, can they pay, and do they actually pay? Reputation covers market and social reputation. Cash flow covers balance sheets, leverage and banking history. The last two tests are about the size of the opportunity: market share gives us volume today, and unsold inventory shows the volume still to come. We start with reputed developers with a large market share, then widen as we build a repayment history.
