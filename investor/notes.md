# Investor deck: founder notes

What the founder has said about the investor deck, so the thinking behind it isn't lost. Read this before changing the deck, and add to it whenever new direction comes in.

Quotes are the founder's words, lightly cleaned where voice dictation garbled them. "My reading" marks an interpretation that hasn't been confirmed.

Secrets and sensitive personal data don't belong here. Team names are included because they're already on the slide.

## The company and the pitch

- **What Aaronz does:** Aaronz handles broker commission payments for developers. The developer pays Aaronz on its usual dates, and Aaronz pays each brokerage. Each brokerage chooses when it is paid and is paid exactly on that day.
- **How it earns:** the usual date is free and earlier dates carry a fee. Proposed fees per AED 100,000: same day 1,500; 15 days 750; 30 days 250; usual date 0. A 50% payment uses half the fee.
- **The pitch angle:** same-day payment, because "for the marketing angle, same day is better".
- **Where it starts:** off-plan commission first, secondary later.
- **AI-native:** the product is to be presented as AI-native: "the product that we are building, where we'll specify that it is AI-native". The specifics are still to come.
- **Developer selection is central:** "even though we are giving the lending and everything, the developer-choosing aspect (like which developers we partner with) is in that." My reading: we are lending, and choosing the right developers is how that lending stays safe. To be confirmed.

## Slide by slide (current direction)

**Slide list.** The original list was: the team, the problem, the solution, the market size, the AI-native product, "how we are making sure", what developers we tie up with, how much we're raising, and where the money goes. The founder then merged "how we make sure" with the developers, merged the amount with where it goes, and approved adding "How we make money".

**Team.** Names only for now: Jikku Luke, Surinder Jaiswani, Bharath Chippa.

**Problem.**
- Timeline: booking is done, meaning the commission milestone is achieved. "It usually takes 4 to 5 days for them to process and tell you, 'Now you can raise an invoice.' Then it takes about 30 to 45 days for them to get the money in their bank account."
- Headline: 2 months "on the top".
- Bigger than the timeline: "We are just stressing on the timeline, but there are other things as well... Let's also stress on the developers... The timeline couldn't be the only thing that's a problem."
- Approved structure: timeline plus three cards for brokerages, developers and a third point.
- Third card: "Nobody fixes it at the source... doesn't make sense. The problem is unsafety." My reading: commission isn't safe until it's paid. Unconfirmed; "uncertainty" is the alternative.

**Solution.**
- "Booking is done (the commission milestone is achieved), and they immediately get notified that now they can withdraw. They can withdraw on the same day."
- Wording: same day, not "within 24 hours".
- The condition: "It is settled on the same day if it is raised before 2 pm."

**Developers.**
- Only developers with a proven track record: "developers who have a proven track record of paying their dues on time, that's what we will emphasise."
- Internal tools: "we have built internal tools that test the developers' records and tell us whether we should fund them or partner with them or not."
- Factors before signing: their reputation, their social reputation, their banking history, their balance sheets, and how leveraged they are. "Considering all these factors, we make a call."
- Wording: "handpicked", not "top-tier", because with top-tier investors might say "you're not capturing the entire market". "It should mean we only work with developers who have a clean track record of financial history." "Reputed developers only" was the other candidate.

**The raise.** "How much we are raising and where the money goes is also on one slide." Numbers are still to come.

**Dates and clocks.** Count from the commission milestone rather than DLD registration, in the investor deck only. The Imtiaz deck stays as it is.
