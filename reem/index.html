Five years of Dubai Land Department sales. The value of property sold went from AED 71.7B in 2020 to AED 680B in 2025, close to ten times. 2020 was the pandemic year, so it is a low starting point, but every year since has grown. The 2021 and 2022 figures are not in the DLD reports we have, so we estimated them from DLD transaction data, adding up every sales registration; that method matches DLD's reported totals for 2023, 2024 and 2025 within 0.5%. Off-plan, where the developer pays the commission, was 63% of sales in 2025.

02The problem · Developers

Developers need a partner to pay commission

Paying every broker on time strains their cash flow, and they want someone to carry it.

Cash flow

Commission strains cash flow

Paying every broker on time ties up cash the project needs.

What they want

A partner who pays it

Someone to pay brokers on time, while the developer pays later.

Smaller developers

Even fewer options

They can't raise funds the way large developers can.

The main problem is that developers have cash flow issues, and they would like a partner who can help them pay commissions. Smaller developers have it worse, because they have fewer ways to raise funds. With a partner paying brokers on time, developers can sell more.

02The problem · Brokers

Brokers wait 4 to 5 months to be paid for a deal

Work starts in month one, but the commission arrives months after the deal closes.

    Month one.

    The deal closes

    The sale is done.

  3. 2 to 3 months

    Commission paid

    4 to 5 months after starting.

Bills

Bills don't wait

Rent and bills are due every month while the commission is still pending.

Moving on

Stuck where they are

A broker who wants to move agency or leave has to wait until the commission is paid.

Indicative timeline.

A broker starts working on a deal in the first month, and it takes about 2 months to close. The commission comes 2 to 3 months after that, so they wait 4 to 5 months in total, while their bills keep coming. A broker who wants to move to another agency or leave has to wait until the commission is paid.

02The problem · Brokerages

Brokerages can't plan, and can't demand their money

They don't know when the commission will land, and only the biggest can push developers to pay.

Planning

Can't plan or grow

With no date for the money, growth plans have to wait.

Smaller brokerages

Can't demand payment

Bigger brokerages can push developers to pay. Smaller ones can't, and many don't survive.

Campaigns

Money sooner means more sales

Every brokerage wants the money earlier, to spend more on campaigns and sell more for developers.

The main problem for brokerages is that they can't plan or grow, because they don't know when they will receive the money. Smaller brokerages can't demand money from the developer the way bigger brokerages do, so many of them die. Bigger brokerages also want the money earlier, so they can spend more on campaigns and get more sales for the developers.

03The solution

A finance partner pays the brokerage the same day

Aaronz is the software. The partner approves each invoice, pays from its own account, and the developer repays the partner.

  1. Developer confirms the commission

    The buyer milestone is met.

  2. Same day

    The partner pays the brokerage

    Its own credit decision, from its own account.

  3. 30 to 45 days

    The developer repays the partner

    The full invoice, on its usual dates.

Worked example: AED 100,000 invoice, repaid after 45 days. Rates are a year on the invoice amount for the days funded (actual/365). The money never passes through Aaronz's bank.

Aaronz holds no lending licence, so a licensed partner is the lender. Aaronz checks the invoice and routes it to the partner. The partner approves it and pays the brokerage from its own account, the developer repays the partner in 30 to 45 days, and Aaronz then invoices the partner its fee. On AED 100,000 repaid in 45 days, the brokerage pays AED 2,219.18 for being paid early: AED 1,479.45 stays with the partner and AED 739.73 is Aaronz's fee. Late interest belongs to the partner.
