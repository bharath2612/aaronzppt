## Problems and fixes

- **Duplicate id:** a slide id `next` clashed with the footer's `#next` button. Renamed it `next-steps`.
- **White slides:** `reveal.css` paints `.reveal-viewport` white, so the dark slides rendered white under white text. Set the viewport background explicitly.
- **Card bullets:** `.horizons li:first-child` also matched nested bullets, making them white on a light card. Scoped the selector to direct children.
- **Labels not uppercase:** a base `.reveal p {text-transform:none}` rule overrode the monospace labels. Added `!important`.
- **Cover gradient:** it showed an edge band; the user then chose a plain white cover.
- **Aaronz & Co logo:** the only source is a low-resolution PNG (291×156). It's cropped and shown at 76px high, so it may look soft on Retina screens.
