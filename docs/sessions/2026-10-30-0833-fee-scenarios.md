Show what Aaronz earns per AED 100K for 30, 60 and 90-day developer payment durations, first as an Excalidraw diagram and then as an editable deck slide.

A copy is in `~/Downloads`.
- New deck slide `#scenarios` "Fee scenarios" (slide 9 of 26), placed after the calculator. Logic is in `assets/scenarios.js`, with styles appended to `assets/presentation.css`.

  - Rows: fee, we earn, we pay the bank, left for us, + fixed fee, net revenue.
  - Formula: net revenue = advance × (our rate − bank rate) × days ÷ 360 + fixed fee, with 100% of the commission advanced.
  - Defaults give net revenue of AED 500 / 500 / 0.
- Mobile: the inputs stack and the table scrolls sideways inside a wrapper.

## Decisions

- One bank rate is shared by all three scenarios; our rate can be edited separately for each.
- The fixed fee is in AED per deal (per AED 100K advanced) and is added after "left for us".
- The 90-day fee at 12% is 3%, not the 3.5% the user estimated (3.5% needs 14%). Kept at 12% as stated; at a 12% bank rate the 90-day case nets AED 0, which was flagged to the user.
